CHI 2026 · Proceedings article

Words to Describe What I’m Feeling: Exploring the Potential of AI Agents for High Subjectivity Decisions in Advance Care Planning

Kellie Yu Hui Sim, Pin Sym Foong, Chenyu Zhao, Melanie Yi Ning Quek, Swarangi Subodh Mehta, and Kenny Tsu Wei Choo.

🏆 Honorable Mention Award

DOI

Loss of decisional capacity, coupled with the increasing absence of reliable human proxies, raises urgent questions about how individuals’ values can be represented in Advance Care Planning (ACP). To probe this fraught design space of high-risk, high-subjectivity decision support, we built an experience prototype (ACPAgent) and asked 15 participants in 4 workshops to train it to be their personal ACP proxy. We analysed their coping strategies and feature requests and mapped the results onto axes of agent autonomy and human control. Our findings show a surprising 86.7% agreement with ACPAgent, arguing for a potential new role of AI in ACP where agents act as personal advocates for individuals, building mutual intelligibility over time. We propose that the key areas of future risk that must be addressed are the moderation of users’ expectations and designing accountability and oversight over agent deployment and cutoffs.

You Cannot Optimize What You Cannot Measure: Multitasking Evaluation as the Missing Foundation of AI-Mediated Heads-Up Interaction

Nuwan Janaka, Runze Cai, Yang Chen, Chenyu Zhao, and Shengdong Zhao

AHIMR 2026 · AI-Mediated Heads-Up Interaction Workshop

Accepted
arXiv

AI-mediated heads-up augmented reality (AR) replaces fixed interfaces with dynamically adapting ones that decide what information to present, in what form, and when, based on a continually changing context that cannot be fully anticipated beforehand. Although it remains an interface, its behavior over time is only partially specified at design time. We argue that this shift requires a corresponding change in evaluation: from snapshots to trajectories. A fixed interface is evaluated in a snapshot—one context, one session, and one set of task-performance metrics. A fluid interface must be evaluated over a trajectory—a sequence of contexts with transitions, sampled from the distribution the interface will actually encounter, and tracked long enough for user trust to form, evolve, and potentially deteriorate. Drawing on the literature for heads-up AR multitasking enabled by optical see-through head-mounted displays (OST-HMDs), we find that current evaluation practice remains largely snapshot-based. Most studies use fixed-condition, single-session designs; interference between concurrent tasks is rarely quantified directly; and commonly used workload measures cannot disentangle cognitive load attributable to individual tasks. To address these limitations, we argue for three shifts: from isolated metrics to Performance Operating Characteristic (POC) interference frontiers, from fixed conditions to evaluation over context trajectories, and from single-session snapshots to longitudinal trust measurement.

AI agents · Smart glasses · Caregiving

AICare: An AI-Driven Head-Mounted Assistant Enhancing Personalized Care for the Elderly

Singapore has a large elderly population, and many families rely on caregivers to support their older family members. To help caregivers manage their daily responsibilities more effectively while building closer connections with the people they care for, we designed a smart glasses prototype based on AiSee.

GitHub repository

Conversation Understanding: Uses WhisperX to transcribe conversations between caregivers and older adults and distinguish between speakers.

Vocal Tone Analysis: Uses Wav2Vec2 to identify emotional cues in the older adult’s tone of voice.

Facial Expression Analysis: Uses camera input and a vision model to analyze the older adult’s initial facial expressions.

Context-Aware Guidance: Integrates conversational, vocal, and visual results through GPT-4o to generate actionable caregiving suggestions.

Schedule Reminders: Delivers voice reminders to caregivers at the scheduled time for the caregiving task.

Navigation · Public spaces

Portable Navigation for Target Booth Locations at Fairgrounds

A portable navigation concept designed to help attendees find target booths in crowded exhibition environments. It uses simple arrow-based cues to support rapid orientation, reduce repeated screen checking, and minimize visual strain in busy spaces.

Research: Surveyed 35 participants to identify navigation challenges in crowded venues.

Design: Co-designed interaction flows in Figma, product form models in Rhinoceros 3D, and a user story map in Miro.

Evaluation: Tested the prototype with six participants.

Machine learning · Image classification

Image Classification Based on Fully Self-Attention Network and Feature Fusion

A research project exploring the potential of Self-Attention Networks (SANet) for image classification. The study compared three network architectures and four convolutional modules, and introduced feature extraction and fusion across the self-attention layers of SANet19. Evaluations on two public datasets showed that feature fusion could improve accuracy, recall, and other performance metrics.

Intelligent product concept · Multimodal interaction · Adaptive environments

Intelligent Air Conditioning

A smart air-conditioning concept featuring voice and regional-dialect recognition, intelligent sleep-mode adjustment for comfort and energy saving, location-based atmosphere simulation, and an integrated projection function.

Voice Control

The smart air conditioner would recognize users’ voices and respond to both direct commands and natural expressions such as “too cold” or “too hot.” It would also support regional dialects to accommodate users from different regions.

Automatic Sleep Mode Recognition

When users forget to turn off the air conditioner or set a timer, the system automatically enters sleep mode to reduce unnecessary electricity use. It would also monitor indoor temperature and humidity, recognize users’ facial characteristics, and adjust the conditions to suit the sleeping needs of different age groups. This aims to support comfortable sleep while reducing unnecessary energy consumption in regions with year-round hot weather and frequent air-conditioner use.

Atmosphere Adjustment

When users enter a specific longitude and latitude, the air conditioner would simulate the environmental conditions of that location. This allows users to experience the atmosphere of another place, such as their hometown, while living elsewhere.

Projection Function

By integrating a projector lens, the air conditioner could also function as a projector, reducing the need for an additional device and saving space.

Web platform · Route planning · E-commerce

Visual Logistics Service System Based on Route Planning

A web-based platform that uses artificial intelligence and Internet technologies to simulate consumer purchasing and supplier delivery management. In particular, users can enter a starting point and destination and observe the truck’s simulated route, making the transportation process more transparent and helping them clearly understand the shipment’s current location and the remaining distance to the destination.

Deep learning · Public health

Face Recognition of Individuals Wearing Masks Based on Deep Learning

During the COVID-19 pandemic, people were required to wear masks in public, yet facial recognition was still necessary in certain situations. In response, we developed a facial-recognition system designed to identify people while they were wearing masks. The project aimed to make identity verification in public places, such as airports, more convenient while reducing the risk of infection associated with removing masks.
